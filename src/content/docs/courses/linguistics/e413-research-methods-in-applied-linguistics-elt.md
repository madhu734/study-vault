# E 413: Research Methods in Applied Linguistics and ELT

## 🎯 Course Objective
Familiarizes students with theoretical and practical research facets in ELT and applied linguistics, equipping them to critically read literature, design studies, and complete the 4th-year dissertation.

---

## 📊 Syllabus Topics & Research Modules

### 1. Research Concepts & Paradigms
- Qualitative, Quantitative, and Mixed Methods paradigms

### 2. Research Methods in Applied Linguistics & ELT
- **Narrative Inquiry**
- **Case Study**
- **Action Research** (Plan $ightarrow$ Act $ightarrow$ Observe $ightarrow$ Reflect)
- **Grounded Theory Method**
- **Survey Research** (Questionnaire design, Likert scales, validity, reliability)
- **Experimental Research** (Pre-test/post-test control group designs)
- **Corpus Linguistics Research**
- **Digital Discourse Research**
- **Discourse Analysis (DA) & Critical Discourse Analysis (CDA)**

### 3. Formulating Research Design
- Problem Statement & Justification
- Research Objectives, Questions (RQs), and Hypotheses ($H_0$ vs. $H_1$)
- Operational Definitions and Study Limitations

### 4. Literature Review
- Functions, thematic synthesis, avoiding cataloging

### 5. Methodology, Sampling & Statistics
- **Sampling:** Probability (random, stratified) vs. Non-probability (purposive, convenience, snowball)
- **Instrumentation & Triangulation** (Data, investigator, methodological)
- **Data Collection:** Interviews, observations, think-aloud protocols
- **Statistical Analysis:** Descriptive (mean, standard deviation) and Inferential (Chi-Square $\chi^2$, Pearson $r$, $t$-test, ANOVA, Factor Analysis)

### 6. Presentation of Findings & Discussion
- Data visualization (charts, thematic matrices), linking findings to theory

### 7. Documentation & Ethics
- Research ethics, informed consent, plagiarism prevention
- **APA Style (7th Edition):** In-text citations and reference list formatting

### 8. Dissertation & Proposal Writing
- Structures of research papers, dissertations, and research proposals

---
