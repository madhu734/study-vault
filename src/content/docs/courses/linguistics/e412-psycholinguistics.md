## 🎯 Course Objective
Explores cognitive and neurological mechanisms underlying language production, comprehension, and acquisition, examining L1 and L2 development and individual differences.

---

## 🧠 Syllabus Topics & Study Modules

### 1. Introduction & Foundational Scopes
- Definition, history, development, and investigative methods in psycholinguistics

### 2. Child Language Acquisition (L1 Stages)
- **Pre-linguistic Stage:** Crying, cooing, vegetative sounds
- **Babbling Stage:** Canonical and variegated babbling
- **Sensori-motor Stage**
- **Holophrastic Stage (One-word):** Overextension and underextension
- **Two-word Stage:** Semantic relations
- **Telegraphic Stage:** Emergence of syntax and morphemes

### 3. L1 Acquisition Theories
- **Behaviourist Theory (B. F. Skinner):** Stimulus-Response-Reinforcement; Chomsky's critique
- **Mentalist / Innatist Theory (Noam Chomsky):** LAD, Universal Grammar, Poverty of the Stimulus
- **Biological Theory (Eric Lenneberg):** Biological foundations, brain lateralization, Critical Period
- **Cognitive Theory (Jean Piaget):** Stages of cognitive development
- **Sociological / Interactionist Theory (Vygotsky, Bruner):** ZPD, LASS, social scaffolding

### 4. Second Language Acquisition (L2 Theories)
- **Stephen Krashen’s Monitor Model:**
  1. *Acquisition-Learning Hypothesis*
  2. *Natural Order Hypothesis*
  3. *Monitor Hypothesis*
  4. *Input Hypothesis ($i+1$)*
  5. *Affective Filter Hypothesis*
- **Interlanguage Theory (Larry Selinker):** Systemic variability, error analysis, fossilization
- **Linguistic Universals & UG in SLA:** Access to UG debates
- **Acculturation Theory (John Schumann):** Social and psychological distance
- **Cognitive Information Processing:** Automaticity, working memory

### 5. Individual Factors in L2
- Age (Critical Period Hypothesis in SLA)
- Language Aptitude (MLAT framework)
- Attitude & Motivation (Gardner, Dörnyei's L2 Motivational Self System)
- Personality: Extroversion/Introversion, anxiety, self-esteem, inhibition
- Cognitive styles: Field independence vs. dependence
- Memory & Transfer of training (positive vs. negative transfer)
- Linguistic Fossilization

### 6. Neurolinguistics & Language Pathologies
- Hemispheric specialization, Broca’s Area, Wernicke’s Area, Arcuate Fasciculus
- **Aphasia:** Broca’s Aphasia, Wernicke’s Aphasia, Conduction Aphasia
- Dyslexia and Language Impairments
- Speech and language therapy principles
