## 🎯 Course Objective
Introduces students to contemporary critical and cultural theories, offering groundbreaking critical schools from formalism to postcolonialism, enabling students to interrogate literary and popular discourses.

---

## 🧭 Critical Schools & Theoretical Modules

### 1. Formalism and New Criticism
- **Defamiliarization (*Ostranenie*):** Viktor Shklovsky
- **Foregrounding:** Vladimir Mayakovsky
- **Practical Criticism:** I. A. Richards

### 2. Structuralism and Semiotics
- **Sign and Signification:** Ferdinand de Saussure (*signifier*, *signified*, arbitrariness)
- **Syntagm and Paradigm:** Syntagmatic vs. associative relations
- **Binary Oppositions:** Claude Lévi-Strauss
- **Archetypal Criticism & Mythoi:** Northrop Frye
- **Narratology:** Tzvetan Todorov
- **‘Mythology’:** Roland Barthes

### 3. Reader-Oriented Theories
- **Hermeneutics & Phenomenology**
- **Horizon of Expectations:** Hans Robert Jauss
- **Reception Theory & Implied Reader:** Wolfgang Iser
- **Speech Act Theory:** J. L. Austin & John Searle

### 4. Marxist Literary Theory
- **Dialectical Materialism:** Karl Marx & Friedrich Engels
- **Base and Superstructure**
- **Ideology, Alienation & Commodity Fetishism**
- **Cultural Hegemony:** Antonio Gramsci
- **Ideological State Apparatuses (ISA):** Louis Althusser

### 5. Feminist Literary Theory
- **Androgyny:** Virginia Woolf (*A Room of One's Own*)
- **The 'Other':** Simone de Beauvoir (*The Second Sex*)
- **'Images of Women' Criticism:** Kate Millett (*Sexual Politics*)
- **Gynocriticism:** Elaine Showalter
- **L’écriture féminine:** Hélène Cixous
- **Black Feminist Criticism:** Barbara Smith
- **Postcolonial Feminism:** Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak

### 6. Psychoanalytic Criticism
- **Freudian Concepts:** Oedipal Complex, Unconscious, Drives & Defenses, Dream-Work (*condensation, displacement*)
- **Object Relations Theory**
- **Lacanian Psychoanalysis:** The Imaginary, Symbolic, and Real; Mirror Stage; The Gaze
- **Trauma Theory**

### 7. Post-structuralism and Deconstruction
- **Logocentrism, *Différance*, and Aporia:** Jacques Derrida
- **Jouissance & The Semiotic Chora:** Julia Kristeva
- **The Death of the Author:** Roland Barthes
- **Rhizome, Deterritorialization, and Becoming:** Gilles Deleuze & Félix Guattari
- **Disciplinarity & Panopticism:** Michel Foucault

### 8. Postmodernism
- **The Postmodern Condition & Grand Narrative Collapse:** Jean-François Lyotard
- **Intertextuality:** Julia Kristeva
- **Hyperreality & Simulacra:** Jean Baudrillard
- **Pastiche vs. Parody:** Fredric Jameson
- **Marxist Critique:** Terry Eagleton

### 9. Postcolonialism
- **Orientalism & Othering:** Edward Said
- **Hybridity, Mimicry & Third Space:** Homi K. Bhabha
- **Double Consciousness:** W. E. B. Du Bois & Frantz Fanon
- **The Subaltern:** Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak (*"Can the Subaltern Speak?"*)
- **Neocolonialism**

### 10. Cultural Theory
- **Culture as Everyday Practice:** Raymond Williams
- **Popular Culture & Consumption**
- **Encoding/Decoding & Representation:** Stuart Hall
