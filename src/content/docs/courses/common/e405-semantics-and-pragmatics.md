# E 405: Semantics and Pragmatics

## 🎯 Course Objective
Explores **Semantics** (literal, context-free meaning) and **Pragmatics** (context-dependent meaning in actual use). Equips students to analyze language functions from linguistic and philosophical perspectives.

---

## 🔍 Section A: Semantics

### 1. Introduction to Meaning
- Defining meaning; semiotic triangle (Symbol, Thought, Referent)
- Levels of analysis (lexical, sentential, utterance)
- Conceptual (denotative) vs. Associative (connotative, affective, stylistic) meaning

### 2. Key Notions in Semantics
- **Sense, Reference, and Denotation**
- **Proposition, Sentence, and Utterance**
- Ambiguity: Lexical vs. Structural ambiguity
- Componential Analysis (*semantic markers*)
- Semantic / Thematic Roles: *Agent, Patient, Theme, Experiencer, Instrument, Beneficiary, Goal, Source*
- Lexical Sense Relations:
  - *Synonymy, Antonymy (gradable, complementary, relational)*
  - *Hyponymy and Hypernymy*
  - *Homonymy, Polysemy, and Metonymy*

### 3. Theories of Meaning
- Structural, Behaviourist (Bloomfield), Cognitive (Prototypes), Truth-Conditional (Frege, Tarski), and Generative Semantics

### 4. Figurative Language
- Conceptual Metaphor Theory (Lakoff & Johnson)
- Irony, Metonymy, and Polysemy

---

## 💬 Section B: Pragmatics

### 1. Scope & Foundations
- Entailment vs. Presupposition (presupposition triggers, constancy under negation)

### 2. Implicature & Gricean Maxims
- Conventional vs. Conversational Implicature
- **The Cooperative Principle (H. P. Grice)**:
  1. *Quantity*
  2. *Quality*
  3. *Relation*
  4. *Manner*
- Observing, flouting, violating, and infringing maxims

### 3. Deixis and Indexicality
- Deictic centre (*origo*) and deictic projection
- Types: *Person, Time, Place, Discourse, Social* (Honorifics)

### 4. Speech Act Theory
- J. L. Austin: *Performatives vs. Constatives*; Felicity conditions
- *Locutionary*, *Illocutionary*, and *Perlocutionary* acts
- **John Searle’s Typology of Speech Acts:**
  1. *Assertives / Representatives*
  2. *Directives*
  3. *Commissives*
  4. *Expressives*
  5. *Declarations*
- Direct vs. Indirect Speech Acts

### 5. Politeness Theory
- Erving Goffman’s concept of **Face**
- **Brown and Levinson's Model:**
  - *Negative Face* vs. *Positive Face*
  - Face Threatening Acts (FTAs) & Politeness strategies
- Impoliteness models (Jonathan Culpeper)

### 6. Conversational Analysis (CA)
- Turn-taking mechanisms
- Adjacency pairs and preference organization
- Insertion sequences and repair mechanisms
- Topic management

### 7. Discourse Dimensions
- Semantics-Pragmatics interface
- **Cohesion (Halliday & Hasan)** vs. **Coherence**
