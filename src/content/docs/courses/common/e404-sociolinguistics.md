# E 404: Sociolinguistics

## 🎯 Course Objective
Provides students with a sound understanding of language in relation to society, investigating the mutual relationship between social structure and linguistic behaviour.

---

## 📋 Syllabus Topics & Study Modules

### 1. Scope and Foundations
- Definition and scope of sociolinguistics
- Sociolinguistics vs. Sociology of Language (micro vs. macro)

### 2. Language and Dialect
- Language vs. dialect: Mutual intelligibility, power, and prestige
- Standard language ideology and the 4 steps of standardization:
  1. *Selection*
  2. *Codification*
  3. *Elaboration of function*
  4. *Acceptance*

### 3. Contact Languages: Pidgin, Creole & Continuum
- Pidgin definition, structural characteristics, and pidginization
- Nativization and creolization
- The Post-Creole Continuum: *Basilect*, *Mesolect*, and *Acrolect*

### 4. Language and Geography
- Regional variation, dialect geography
- **Wave Theory** and **Isogloss** (bundle of isoglosses)

### 5. Multilingualism and Diglossia
- Individual bilingualism vs. societal multilingualism
- **Diglossia (Ferguson & Fishman):** High (H) vs. Low (L) varieties, functional compartmentalization
- Extended diglossia in postcolonial contexts

### 6. Code-Switching, Code-Mixing, and Borrowing
- **Code-Switching (CS):** Situational vs. Metaphorical (Gumperz); Markedness Model (Myers-Scotton)
- **Code-Mixing (CM):** Intra-sentential insertion
- Borrowing vs. Switching
- Syntactic constraints: Free Morpheme Constraint and Equivalence Constraint (Poplack)

### 7. Language Planning and Policy (LPP)
- Status planning, Corpus planning, Acquisition planning
- Linguistic ideologies (assimilation, pluralism, vernacularization, internationalism)
- Case studies (Bangladesh, post-colonial nations)

### 8. Language Variation and Change
- Social variables: Class, age, ethnicity, social networks
- William Labov's sociolinguistic methodology (Martha's Vineyard, NYC department stores)
- Sound change and lexical diffusion

### 9. Language, Culture & Thought
- Linguistic Relativity and Determinism (**Sapir-Whorf Hypothesis**)
- Cultural representation and Stuart Hall's Representation Theory

### 10. Language and Gender / Identity
- Deficit, Dominance, and Difference models (Lakoff, Spender, Tannen)
- Performativity of gender (Judith Butler)
- Linguistic identity construction

### 11. Global Englishes & Hegemony
- **Linguistic Imperialism (Robert Phillipson):** Center vs. Periphery
- **Language Fossilization:** Causes and manifestations in SLA
