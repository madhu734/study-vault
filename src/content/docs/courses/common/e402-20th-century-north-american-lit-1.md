## 🎯 Course Objective & Context
This course samples major North American works in English of the 20th century. Students will explore stylistic, structural, and narrative trends across key historical periods:
- **The Roaring Twenties**
- **The Great Depression**
- **The Lost Generation**
- **The American Dream & Its Disillusionment**
- **Racial Trauma & The Southern Gothic**

## 📝 Critical Focus & Exam Questions
1. **Pastoralism and Anxiety in Frost:** The tension between natural harmony and modern human isolation.
2. **Confessional Poetics:** Sylvia Plath's subversive poetic persona, feminist rage, and resurrection motifs.
3. **The Disillusioned American Dream:** Comparative study of Willy Loman (*Death of a Salesman*) and James Tyrone (*Long Day’s Journey into Night*).
4. **Hemingway's Iceberg Theory:** Omission and subtext in *"A Clean, Well-lighted Place"*.
