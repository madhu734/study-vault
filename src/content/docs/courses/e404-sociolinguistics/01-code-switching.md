# Code-Switching and Code-Mixing

## 1. Core Definitions

> [!NOTE]
> **Code-Switching (CS):** The juxtaposition within the same speech exchange of passages of speech belonging to two different grammatical systems or subsystems (Gumperz, 1982).

> **Code-Mixing (CM):** The embedding of various linguistic units such as affixes, words, phrases, and clauses from two distinct grammatical systems within a single sentence or utterance.

### Comparison Table

| Feature | Code-Switching (CS) | Code-Mixing (CM) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Linguistic Level** | Inter-sentential or discourse level | Intra-sentential (within clause/sentence) |
| **Grammatical Integrity** | Both codes retain independent syntax | One base language accommodates guest elements |
| **Purpose** | Pragmatic shift, audience design, topic shift | Expressive nuance, lexical gap-filling |

---

## 2. Theoretical Frameworks

### A. John J. Gumperz (1982)
- **Situational Code-Switching:** Driven by external context (change in participants, setting, or formality).
- **Metaphorical Code-Switching:** Driven by communicative intent (redefining social distance, solidarity, irony, or authority).

### B. Carol Myers-Scotton: The Markedness Model
- Speakers evaluate choices as **unmarked** (expected norm) or **marked** (negotiating a new social role).

## 3. Exam Focus: Critical Discussion

### Q: "Code-switching is a sign of communicative competence rather than linguistic deficiency." Discuss.
* **Key Argument:** Early deficit models viewed switching as a breakdown in grammar. Modern sociolinguistics (Poplack, Gumperz) demonstrates that switching strictly respects syntactic constraints (Equivalence Constraint & Free Morpheme Constraint), requiring high proficiency in both languages.

## 4. Viva Voce Preparation

**Q1: What is the Free Morpheme Constraint proposed by Shana Poplack?**  
> *Answer:* A switch cannot occur between a bound morpheme and a lexical item unless the lexical item has been phonologically integrated into the host language.

**Q2: How does diglossia differ from code-switching?**  
> *Answer:* Diglossia is a societal, institutionalized compartmentalization of High and Low varieties, whereas code-switching is an interactive, individual communicative strategy.
