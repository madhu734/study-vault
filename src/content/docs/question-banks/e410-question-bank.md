### Syllabus Topics

- **Foundations:** Phonetics vs. Phonology; Speech production organs; Airstream mechanisms (pulmonic egressive, glottalic, velaric).
- **Phonological Concepts:** Phonemes, phones, and allophones (Trubetzkoy, Jakobson, Daniel Jones, Chomsky, Halle); Minimal pairs and sets.
- **Vowel System:** English monophthongs (classification by tongue height, backness, and lip rounding); Diphthongs and triphthongs; Cardinal vowel chart.
- **Consonant System:** English consonants categorized by place (bilabial, labiodental, dental, alveolar, post-alveolar, palatal, velar, glottal) and manner of articulation (plosives, fricatives, affricates, nasals, liquids/approximants).
- **Syllable Structure:** Phonetic and phonological definitions; Syllable template (Onset, Rhyme: Nucleus/Peak, Coda); Phonotactic constraints and possibilities.
- **Connected Speech:** Strong and weak forms; Contractions; Elision; Assimilation (progressive, regressive, coalescent); Linking.
- **Suprasegmentals:** Stress (nature, acoustic correlates, stress rules on simple/compound words); Stress-timed vs. syllable-timed rhythm; Tone and tone units; Tone sandhi; Intonation and its communicative functions.
- **Transcription:** Broad and narrow IPA transcription of isolated words and connected spoken discourse.
