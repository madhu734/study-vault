### সিলেবাসের বিষয়বস্তু (Syllabus Topics)

- **Research Concepts & Paradigms:** Definition, nature, ethics, and significance of research; Quantitative, Qualitative, and Mixed-Methods paradigms; Post-positivism, Pragmatism, Participatory paradigms.
- **Research Designs & Traditions:** Narrative Inquiry; Qualitative & Quantitative Case Study; Action Research (Cycles: plan, act, observe, reflect); Grounded Theory; Survey Research (cross-sectional, longitudinal); Experimental & Quasi-experimental designs (pretest-posttest, control groups); Corpus Research; Discourse Analysis & Critical Discourse Analysis (CDA); Ethnography.
- **Research Formulation:** Research problem identification; Objectives; Justification; Research questions vs. hypotheses; Variables (independent, dependent, confounding, extraneous); Delimitations and limitations.
- **Literature Review:** Purpose, search strategies, critical synthesis, and step-by-step procedures.
- **Methodology & Instrumentation:** Sampling techniques (probability vs. non-probability); Instrumentation (questionnaire construction, open/closed items; structured, semi-structured, and unstructured interviews; focus group discussions; observation); Triangulation; Member check.
- **Data Analysis & Statistics:** Descriptive statistics (mean, median, standard deviation); Inferential statistics (Pearson Product-moment correlation r, independent/paired samples T-test, One-way ANOVA, Chi-square, Factor analysis).
- **Documentation & Academic Writing:** Plagiarism detection and prevention; Academic integrity; APA style (7th edition) Reference Lists and In-text citations; Research proposal and dissertation structuring.

### Statistical Problem: One-Way ANOVA Hypothesis Testing
`[Batch: 49]`

Problem: You are provided with the test scores of students taught using three different teaching methods:
Method A: 81, 83, 82, 76, 80 (N■ = 5)
Method B: 87, 85, 86, 88, 90 (N■ = 5)
Method C: 91, 92, 93, 95, 94 (N■ = 5)
Task:
1. Conduct a One-Way Analysis of Variance (ANOVA) test to determine if there are statistically significant differences between the
teaching methods.
2. Calculate Between-Group Sum of Squares (SSB), Within-Group Sum of Squares (SSW), Degrees of Freedom (df■ = k-1, df■ =
N-k), Mean Squares (MSB, MSW), and the F-ratio (F = MSB / MSW).
3. Compare the calculated F-ratio to the critical F-value for α = 0.05 from the F-distribution table (df■=2, df■=12, Critical F = 3.89) and
conclude whether the null hypothesis of equal population means can be rejected.
4. State all assumptions made (normality, homogeneity of variance, independence of observations).
