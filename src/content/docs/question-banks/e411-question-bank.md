### Syllabus Topics

#### Morphology

- **Core Concepts:** Definition, classification, functions; Words vs. Lexemes; Wordhood tests; Types of words.
- **Word Formation Processes:** Affixation (prefixes, suffixes, infixes); Bases & roots; Derivation vs. Conversion; Compounding; Backformation; Blending; Clipping; Eponyms; Productivity; Mental lexicon.
- **Morphological Analysis:** Morphological rules and trees; Syntagmatic & paradigmatic relations; Morpheme, morph, and allomorph; Zero morph; Prosodic morphology.
- **Linguistic Interfaces:** Morphophonology, Morphosyntax, and Morphosemantics.
#### Syntax

- **Syntactic Categories & Word Classes:** Verbs, nouns, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions; Lexical vs. Functional categories; Syntactic properties.
- **Constituent Structure:** Noun Phrase (NP head, pre-modifiers, post-modifiers); Verb Phrase (VP, operators, catenatives, tensed vs.
- **non-tensed); Adjective Phrase (AdjP); Adverb Phrase (AdvP:** adjuncts, subjuncts, disjuncts, conjuncts); Prepositional Phrase (PP).
- **Structural Analysis:** Immediate Constituent (IC) analysis; Syntactic constituency tests (movement, replacement, coordination, clefting).
- **Rules & Operations:** Phrase Structure (PS) rules; Tree diagrams; Transformational rules; Merger, projection, and adjunction; X-bar theory; Passivization; Ellipsis.
- **Clause Structure:** Clause components; Subordination vs. Coordination; Tense, aspect, voice, and mood.
- **Theories of Syntax:** Universal Grammar (UG); Government-Binding (GB) theory & Explanatory Adequacy; Lexical-Functional Grammar (LFG); Relational Grammar; Role and Reference Grammar (RRG).

### Morphology Essay Questions
