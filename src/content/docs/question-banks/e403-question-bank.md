### সিলেবাসের বিষয়বস্তু (Syllabus Topics)

#### Core Critical & Cultural Theories

- **Formalism and New Criticism:** Defamiliarization (Shklovsky); foregrounding (Mayakovsky); practical criticism (Richards); close reading.
- **Structuralism and Semiotics:** Sign & signification (Saussure); paradigm & syntagm; binary oppositions; archetypal criticism (Frye); narratology (Todorov); 'mythology' (Barthes).
- **Reader-Oriented Theories:** Hermeneutics; phenomenology; 'horizon of expectations' (Jauss); reception theory (Iser); speech act theory.
- **Marxist Literary Theory:** Dialectic materialism; base & superstructure; ideology; alienation; commodity fetishism; hegemony (Gramsci); ISA & RSA (Althusser).
- **Feminist Literary Theory:** Androgyny (Woolf); Other (Beauvoir); 'Images of Women' (Millett); gynocriticism (Showalter); l'écriture féminine (Cixous); black feminist criticism (Smith, Lorde); postcolonial feminism (Spivak, Mohanty).
- **Psychoanalytic Criticism:** Oedipal complex; the unconscious; drives & defenses; dream-work; Imaginary, Symbolic and Real (Lacan); trauma.
- **Post-structuralism and Deconstruction:** Logocentrism, différance, and aporia (Derrida); jouissance (Kristeva); death of the author (Barthes); rhizome & deterritorialization (Deleuze); power/knowledge & disciplinary discourse (Foucault).
- **Postmodernism:** Grand narrative (Lyotard); hyperreality & simulacra (Baudrillard); pastiche & late capitalism (Jameson).
- **Postcolonialism:** Othering, diaspora, hybridity, double consciousness, subaltern, mimicry, ambivalence (Bhabha, Spivak).
- **Cultural Theory:** Culture; popular culture; consumption; representation (Hall); cultural materialism.
