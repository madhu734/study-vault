### সিলেবাসের বিষয়বস্তু (Syllabus Topics)

- **Foundations:** Definition, history, development, scopes, and multidisciplinary branches of Psycholinguistics.
- **Child Language Acquisition (L1):** Developmental stages (pre-linguistic, babbling, sensori-motor, holophrastic / one-word, two-word, telegraphic); Characteristics of child grammar.
- **L1 Acquisition Theories:** Behaviourist theory (Skinner, conditioning); Mentalist / Innatist theory (Chomsky, LAD, UG); Biological theory & Critical Period Hypothesis (Lenneberg); Cognitive theory (Piaget, cognitive pre-requisites); Social Interactionist theory (Vygotsky, Bruner, LASS, motherese/parentese).
- **Second Language Acquisition (SLA / L2) Theories:** Krashen's Monitor Model (Acquisition-Learning, Natural Order, Monitor, Input, Affective Filter); Interlanguage theory (Selinker, Tarone, Adjemian); Linguistic universals; Schumann's Acculturation Model (social & psychological distance); Cognitive information-processing models.
- **Individual Learner Factors:** Age & Critical Period; Language aptitude; Motivation (integrative vs. instrumental); Attitude; Personality (extroversion, anxiety); Cognitive styles & working memory; Fossilization.
- **Neurolinguistics & Language Pathology:** Hemispheric lateralization; Broca's and Wernicke's areas; Aphasia (Broca's, Wernicke's, conduction); Dyslexia; Language deprivation (Victor, Genie); Language therapy.

## Section A: Broad Essay Questions

> *Comprehensive essay questions from past examinations, organized according to key theoretical areas.*

### 1. Scope of Psycholinguistics & Neurolinguistics

1. **What is psycholinguistics? Give an account of the development, scope, and significance of psycholinguistics as an interdisciplinary field of study.**  
   `[Batches: 50, 49, 47, 46, 43, 42]`

2. **Discuss the relationship between the brain and language. Write on aphasia, its types (Broca's, Wernicke's), causes, and prominent features. Provide clinical and linguistic examples.**  
   `[Batches: 50, 42]`

3. **What role does the human brain play in the language acquisition process? Briefly discuss the lateralization of functions in the human brain.**  
   `[Batches: 43, 42]`

### 2. Child Language Acquisition Stages & L1 Theories

1. **What is language acquisition? Discuss the different stages of a child's L1 acquisition from birth to the age of 30 months (cooing, babbling, holophrastic, two-word, telegraphic).**  
   `[Batches: 49, 48, 47, 44, 43, 42]`

2. **Explain the Nativist / Innatist Theory of 1st language acquisition. Point out the strengths and weaknesses of the theory with reference to Chomsky's LAD and Universal Grammar.**  
   `[Batches: 50, 48, 46, 44, 43, 42]`

3. **What is the behaviourist notion of language acquisition? Substantiate behaviourism as a theoretical approach that facilitates language acquisition with reference to a particular teaching method (e.g., Audio-Lingual Method) built around it.**  
   `[Batches: 49, 48, 47, 46, 44, 43, 42]`

4. **How does the traditional nature-nurture debate relate to the study of language acquisition? Contrast the behaviourist and mentalist positions.**  
   `[Batches: 49, 48, 44]`

5. **Discuss biological theory with its limitations. What evidence supports the biological basis of language?**  
   `[Batch: 47]`

6. **Discuss Piaget's theory of cognitive development in the light of his contention that "development precedes learning."**  
   `[Batch: 42]`

7. **What is motherese (child-directed speech)? Discuss the characteristics of motherese and point out its effects on early language development.**  
   `[Batch: 49]`

### 3. Second Language Acquisition (SLA) Theories

1. **Expound the basic propositions of Krashen's Monitor Model Theory (the 5 hypotheses). Evaluate its merits, methodological flaws, and relevance as a second language learning theory.**  
   `[Batches: 50, 49, 48, 47, 46, 44, 43, 42]`

2. **What is Interlanguage? What are its major characteristics? Discuss the causes, stages, and processes of Interlanguage development (Selinker, Tarone, Adjemian).**  
   `[Batches: 50, 49, 48, 46, 44, 43, 42]`

3. **Evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of Schumann's Acculturation Model in explaining L2 learning. How does social and psychological distance affect SLA?**  
   `[Batches: 50, 47, 46, 44, 43]`

4. **What is Universal Grammar? How would you explain the relationship between UG and second language acquisition?**  
   `[Batches: 46, 44]`

5. **Reflect on your own second language learning experience (English), critically evaluating it using some of the SLA theories that you have studied.**  
   `[Batch: 43]`

### 4. Age Factor, Individual Differences & Fossilization

1. **With reference to different cases of language deprivation (Victor of Aveyron, Genie) and adult learners, present a critical analysis of Lenneberg's Critical Period Hypothesis (CPH).**  
   `[Batches: 50, 49, 48, 46]`

2. **Name the different individual factors that cause variation among learners in second language learning (aptitude, attitude, motivation, cognitive styles, personality). Discuss their roles in detail.**  
   `[Batches: 50, 48, 46, 44, 42]`

3. **Define fossilization. Identify the aspects and causes of fossilization, and discuss how fossilization in an L2 learner's interlanguage continuum challenges linguistic development.**  
   `[Batch: 49]`
